Privacy policy

We respect your privacy. MultiDB is a free and ad-free store, and we do not collect any personal data that could identify you.

To understand how our store is used and to improve it, we measure site visits and the number of downloads using Simple Analytics and GoatCounter. Both tools are privacy-first: they do not use cookies and do not collect personal information.

We do not sell, share or trade your data with third parties. Our servers, websites and files are hosted by Vercel, Catbox and Google Drive.
